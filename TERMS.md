## Nature of the output

Results are screening context drawn from federal public records, each with its publisher and date. They are not a regulatory, insurance, engineering or property determination, and not a determination of status under any federal program. The publishing agency's own service is authoritative. For warnings, evacuations and protective actions, the issuing authority is the source to follow.
